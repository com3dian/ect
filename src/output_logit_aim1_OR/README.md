Extension of Aim 1: Empirical Evaluation of Logical Disjunction (OR) via Categorical Colimits

1. Objective & Theoretical Grounding
Following the validation of logical conjunction (AND) as categorical limits (pointwise minimum), this phase extends the evaluation to logical disjunction (OR). Under the Enriched Category Theory framework, the logical OR operation corresponds to categorical colimits, computationally defined as the pointwise maximum over the copresheaves. The objective is to verify whether the empirical representation of $h^{A \lor B}$ structurally aligns with the theoretical prediction $\max(h^A, h^B)$.

2. Data Efficiency & Control Variables (Prompt Framing)To maintain strict experimental control and maximize computational efficiency, the empirical distributions for the individual concepts ($P_A$ and $P_B$) will be fully inherited from the existing extracted_distributions_top8192.json dataset.To construct the empirical baseline for the disjunction, a new parallel prompt ($P_{A \lor B}$) will be passed through the target model (Qwen/Qwen3.5-1.5B):$P_A$ (Cached): "Describe the typical properties and context of a {word_a}:"$P_B$ (Cached): "Describe the typical properties and context of a {word_b}:"$P_{A \lor B}$ (New): "Describe the typical properties and context of an entity that is either a {word_a} or a {word_b}:"

3. Incremental Extraction ProtocolA lightweight extraction script will be deployed to process only the new $P_{A \lor B}$ prompts. Consistent with the prior methodology, a Logit Lens will be applied at the final token position. The script will perform the identical Top-8192 truncation to isolate the core semantic manifold while eliminating long-tail Softmax noise. These newly extracted distributions will be merged back into the existing JSON structure.

4. Computation of Categorical Colimits & NormalizationFor each concept pair across the 5D Orthogonality Spectrum, the theoretical colimit will be computed as the element-wise maximum of the cached distributions:$$P_{theoretical\_or}(x) = \max(P_A(x), P_B(x))$$Crucial Mathematical Step: Because the pointwise maximum of two probability distributions does not natively sum to 1, the resulting theoretical vector must be re-normalized (divided by its scalar sum) to form a valid probability distribution before divergence metrics can be calculated.

5. Evaluation and VisualizationThe structural divergence (e.g., KL Divergence or Jensen-Shannon Divergence) between the empirical $P_{A \lor B}$ and the normalized theoretical $\max(P_A, P_B)$ will be calculated. We will plot the log-scale error curves for OR (similar to the AND curves) across the 5 categories. Specifically, we will observe if Category 4 (Surreal) and Category 5 (Mutually Exclusive) exhibit different geometric penalty scaling when evaluated under the $\max$ operator compared to the $\min$ operator.
